# What would Tufte do? A page-by-page pass

The question asked of every page (rule 30 in `qa/DESIGN_RULES.md`). Each answer names what changed. Every independent review asks it again, and new answers are added here.

## Home
- **Decoration that encodes nothing goes.** The path drawings were removed. Each path now shows a word-sized strip beside its count: one tick per decision, filled once played. It records the path's length and your progress, and nothing else.
- **Data before chrome.** The hero's demo is a real estimate with its evidence plate, not a picture of one. The "Try one now" label above it was dropped; the question is the heading.
- **No grid of identical boxes.** The paths read as an index with rules between entries. The featured path keeps the one dark surface.
- **Headline in three lines** at desktop width, at a size that leaves room for the lede.

## Play (predict)
- The question is the heading; the forecast claim is the heading and "How likely is it that the study found this?" sits under it, not above.
- Causal diagrams show only the factors, with undirected links, until the reveal (unchanged).

## Reveal
- **The headline states the finding** (the answer title), then the verdict as a sentence ("The evidence surprised you. Score 21"), not a pill above the headline.
- **The chart's own title is the measure** (for example "Variance of contributions after seeing others' choices"), the headline above it is the finding, and the source sits inside the figure, under the bars.
- **Direct labels, no colour keys.** The allocate pairs carry "your points" and "effect the study measured" inside the first pair of bars; delegate chips read "You: …" and "Evidence: …"; option recaps carry "Your call" and "Evidence" on the options themselves.
- **Colour only the bar that proves the headline**; all other bars are grey. Effects that were not significant are outlined, and the caption says so once.
- **No side stripe, no label heading for the manager's line.** "For a manager:" leads the sentence.
- **The theme drawing beside the headline was removed**: it encoded nothing and collided with long titles.
- **Calibration points are not shown on the reveal**: one number with a reference that does not fit every format ("a 50% hedge") taught nothing. The reveal says how sure you were and whether you were right; the record carries calibration.

## Your record
- **The big number carries its unit**: "skill points above guessing", beside the number, with the scale in the sentence under it.
- **Calibration plot**: the grey plot box and the grid were removed; axes, ticks and the diagonal stay, the diagonal is labelled "as sure as right" in place, and each dot is labelled with its number of calls.
- The guessing line in the sparkline is labelled on the side away from the data.

## Library and All decisions
- The principle meters show how many of a principle's decisions you have played; nothing else is drawn.
- The All decisions list shows each forecast's claim, not the generic forecast question.

## Console, projector and phones
- Projector stat row is a fixed four-column grid; delegate tasks show the evidence route under each task as text, no colour key.
- The rank projector numbers the study's measured order and gives the room's average rank beside each option.

## Open questions for the next pass
- The wide reveal's right column under the study card is empty. Tufte would use it for a second comparison at the same scale (for example the class's distribution against the study's value in a live class), not for decoration.
- The estimate band is explained in a caption; Tufte would label it on the axis.
